PLACEHOLDER IMAGES NEEDED FOR WEBSITE PREVIEW
================================================

You need to create and add these images to your assets folder:

1. FAVICON (Browser Tab Icon):
   - assets/favicon.ico (16x16, 32x32, 48x48 - ICO format)
   - assets/favicon-16x16.png (16x16 PNG)
   - assets/favicon-32x32.png (32x32 PNG)
   - assets/apple-touch-icon.png (180x180 PNG for iOS devices)

2. SOCIAL MEDIA PREVIEW (Open Graph Image):
   - assets/og-preview.jpg (1200x630 pixels - JPG format)
   
   This image will appear when your website is shared on:
   - Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter, Discord, etc.
   - WhatsApp, Telegram, etc.
   - Any platform that supports Open Graph

RECOMMENDED SPECIFICATIONS:
- Favicon: Simple, recognizable icon (your initials, a robot icon, etc.)
- og-preview.jpg: 1200x630 pixels, shows your name/title clearly
- All images should be high quality and represent your portfolio well

Once you add these images, your website will show:
- A custom icon in browser tabs
- A preview image when shared on social media
- Better professional appearance across all platforms
